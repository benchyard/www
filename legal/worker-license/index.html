Worker license

Official Benchyard Worker images are free to run but are not open source.

The license permits downloading official public images, internal use, and backups. It does not permit redistribution, sale, modification, sublicensing, or presenting an unofficial image as official. Third-party components keep their licenses.

The authoritative license is shipped in each Worker image. This summary does not replace it. Console and the Worker protocol are separately Apache-2.0 licensed.
