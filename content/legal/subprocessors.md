## 2. Payment services

Airwallex is the intended payment platform described by the owner. Its exact contracting entity, merchant account, applicable notices, processing roles and locations must match the actual seller and checkout. An existing account held by one company is not evidence that a future company may use it as its merchant account. Payment providers may act independently for compliance, fraud prevention, or other specified purposes and as processors for other functions; record the actual arrangement.

## 3. Customer-selected services

Customers may select external coding agents, model services, repository hosts, tools, plugins, and endpoints where permitted. Technical support for a connection does not establish that its provider is a Benchyard subprocessor or that access is authorized for every deployment.

Where a customer independently contracts with and selects a provider, that provider's separate processing is governed by the customer's arrangement. Where Benchyard engages a provider to process data on Benchyard's behalf, include it in the completed inventory and apply the required contractual protections. The classification depends on actual instructions, routing, credential handling, and contracts.

## 4. Changes and acceptance record

For customers with an effective [Data Processing Addendum](data-processing-addendum.md), Benchyard gives at least 30 days' prior written notice of a proposed new or replacement subprocessor and follows the DPA's objection procedure. Updating a public page alone does not replace that notice.

The completed register must record its verification date, responsible approver, applicable deployment, provider contracts and transfer instruments, and the version attached to each accepted DPA. No production provider entry or transfer safeguard has been verified by this template.
