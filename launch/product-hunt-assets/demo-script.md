# Product Hunt demo — 75 seconds

Use only the bundled Acme fixture. Record at 1440p, hide browser bookmarks and
notifications, and export H.264 MP4 plus a captioned WebM.

| Time | Screen | Narration |
|---:|---|---|
| 0–8s | Console overview | “Benchyard is the self-hosted workbench where teams and coding agents share work.” |
| 8–20s | Create an Acme ticket | “Describe the issue once, attach context, and assign it to a Worker.” |
| 20–35s | Job timeline | “The Worker plans and executes in its assigned workspace while Console owns the fenced job state.” |
| 35–49s | Gate and change | “Every change rebases, reruns the configured gate, and records the exact commit.” |
| 49–60s | TODOs, docs, tests | “Tickets, TODOs, documentation, and tests stay in one shared operating surface.” |
| 60–69s | Architecture frame | “Console is Apache-2.0. The free official Worker image runs on your infrastructure.” |
| 69–75s | Install page | “Clone it, verify the signed release, and keep your code and data self-hosted.” |

## Capture checklist

- Record no real names, domains, repositories, tokens, SSH paths, or customer data.
- Show the Acme workspace and deterministic fake-worker path where practical.
- Do not show unsupported providers or imply end-to-end secret encryption.
- End on `https://hero.benchyard.com/`.
