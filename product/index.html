One Console, many Workers

Benchyard is a self-hosted team workspace and job orchestration console, not a hosted SaaS.

The flow

  1. A person creates or steers work in Console.
  2. Console grants an assigned Worker a fenced, expiring job lease.
  3. The Worker runs the job in a dedicated process scoped to its workspace.
  4. Console records events and remains authoritative for workflow state.
  5. A person reviews, verifies, or reverts the result.

Distribution

Console and the Worker contract are Apache-2.0. Official Worker images are free public binaries under the Worker license; Worker source is private.
